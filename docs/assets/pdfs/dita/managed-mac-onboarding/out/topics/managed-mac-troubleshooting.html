Troubleshooting Managed Mac Setup

Use this topic to identify common setup problems and recommended actions.

I cannot sign in

The email address, password, or network connection may be incorrect, or the account may not be ready.

  1. Verify your email address.
    Example: jbob@mycompany.com
  2. Confirm your password is correct and not expired.
  3. Check your Wi-Fi connection.
  4. Restart your Mac and try again.

MFA is not working

The authentication app may not be available, up to date, or presenting a current code.

  1. Check that your authentication app is available and up to date.
  2. Retry the MFA prompt.
  3. Ensure the code is current before entering it.

Zscaler is not connecting

The connection may fail because of incorrect credentials, incomplete MFA, or lack of network connectivity.

  1. Confirm your credentials.
  2. Complete MFA if prompted.
  3. Restart the application.
  4. Check your Wi-Fi connection.

Office apps are missing

Application installation may still be in progress or the apps may need to be installed from Self Service.

  1. Open Self Service to install apps.
  2. Confirm setup has completed.
  3. Wait a few minutes and try again.

Self Service is missing

Enrollment may still be completing or the application may not yet be available on the Mac.

  1. Wait a few minutes for enrollment to complete.
  2. Restart your Mac.
  3. Contact IT if Self Service still does not appear.
