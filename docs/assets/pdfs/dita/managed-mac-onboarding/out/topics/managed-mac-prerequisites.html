Before You Begin

This topic lists the information, devices, and access needed before you begin setup of your managed Mac.

Required items

  • Your company email address
    • Example: jbob@mycompany.com
  • Your company password
  • Your approved MFA method on your iPhone or other iOS device
  • Access to a Wi-Fi network
  • Your Mac power adapter
  • Any onboarding instructions provided by your IT team

Security note

Important:
Do not share your company password or MFA verification code. IT will never ask for this information.
