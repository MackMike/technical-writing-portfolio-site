Connect to Company Services and Install Approved Apps

Use these steps to connect to the secure company Wi-Fi network, verify Microsoft 365 access, and install approved apps from Self Service.

After initial setup is complete, you may need to connect to additional company services and verify access to required applications.

  1. Connect to the secure company Wi-Fi network if you are in a company office or your organization requires it.
    1. Select the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar.
    2. Choose the approved secure company wireless network.
      Example: MyCompany-Secure
    3. Enter your credentials if prompted.
    4. Confirm that the Wi-Fi icon shows an active connection.
  2. Verify access to Microsoft 365 and Office applications.
    1. Open an application such as Outlook or Word.
    2. Sign in with your company account if prompted.
      Example: jbob@mycompany.com
    3. Complete MFA if required.
    4. Confirm that the application opens and your account is recognized.
    If setting up Outlook, make sure Zscaler is connected first if required by your organization.
  3. Open Self Service and install approved apps if needed.
    1. Open Applications.
    2. Select Self Service.
    3. Sign in if prompted.
    4. Browse available applications.
    5. Select the app you need.
    6. Select Install.
    7. Wait for installation to complete.

You have verified access to company services and can install approved applications as needed.

Examples of apps that may be available in Self Service

  • Microsoft Outlook
  • Microsoft Word
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Microsoft Teams
  • Google Chrome
  • Zoom
