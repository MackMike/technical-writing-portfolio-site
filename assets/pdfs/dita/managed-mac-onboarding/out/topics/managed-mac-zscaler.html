Connect to Zscaler

Use these steps to connect Zscaler if your organization requires it for secure access to company resources.

Some organizations use Zscaler to provide secure access to company applications and network resources. Depending on your setup, Zscaler may connect automatically.

  1. Open the Zscaler application if it does not open automatically.
  2. Sign in with your company account.
    Example: jbob@mycompany.com
  3. Complete MFA if prompted.
  4. Confirm that Zscaler shows a connected or active status.

Zscaler is connected and secure access to required company resources is available.

Warning:
Some company applications and network resources may not be available until Zscaler is connected.
