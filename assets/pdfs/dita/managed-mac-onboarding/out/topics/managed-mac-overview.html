Overview of Managed Mac Setup

This topic explains what users can expect during setup of a company-managed Mac and what tasks are typically completed during onboarding.

This guide helps employees set up and begin using a company-managed Mac. It covers common onboarding tasks such as connecting to Wi-Fi, signing in, completing multi-factor authentication (MFA), allowing automated setup to finish, connecting to Zscaler, and accessing company applications such as Microsoft Outlook and other Microsoft 365 tools.

Tip:
Estimated setup time: 20 to 30 minutes.

What this guide helps you do

  • Sign in to your Mac with your company account
  • Connect to Wi-Fi
  • Complete MFA using your approved iOS authentication app
  • Allow automated enrollment and setup to finish
  • Connect to Zscaler
  • Access Microsoft 365 and local Office applications
  • Use Self Service to install approved apps

What happens during managed setup

When you turn on a new or reissued company-managed Mac, the device may automatically enroll in your organization’s device management system. In many enterprise environments, this process is supported by Apple Business Manager and a management platform such as Jamf.

During setup, your Mac may automatically apply company settings, security controls, certificates, applications, and other required configurations. Some tasks may complete in the background after you sign in.
